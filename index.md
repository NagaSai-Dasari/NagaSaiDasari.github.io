Exploring the frontiers of software engineering with AI. This blog dives into the AI4SWEng project, its innovative architecture, and how AI-driven orchestration is transforming the way complex software systems are built.

We, University of Reading (UREAD) are one of the 15 partners contributing to the AI4SWEng project, collaborating on research and development of AI-assisted, enterprise-grade software engineering tools and methodologies.

This blog covers the AI4SWEng framework architecture, including KIOs, orchestration, and human-in-the-loop integration, highlighting how AI can accelerate, secure, and govern modern software development.
